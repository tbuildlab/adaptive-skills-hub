## 1. Question, baseline, and conclusion

This review asks whether the accepted G2 Host-qualification obligations confuse Nexus product properties with replaceable proof techniques. It evaluates the accepted [Roadmap G2](nexus-roadmap-v2.md), [Pre-G2 A0–A6](nexus-pre-g2-architecture-addendum-v1.md) and [acceptance](nexus-pre-g2-architecture-acceptance-v1.md), [G1](nexus-v2-g1.md), [HQ Scope & Exit](nexus-g2-host-qualification-scope-and-exit-decision-v1.md) and [acceptance](nexus-g2-host-qualification-scope-and-exit-acceptance-v1.md), [Next Host Route Decision](nexus-g2-next-host-route-decision-v1.md), and the accepted Route B [P1–P12 contract](nexus-g2-route-b-provider-neutral-host-contract-v1.md) and [acceptance](nexus-g2-route-b-provider-interface-acceptance-v1.md). R2/R4/R5 are read only at their frozen integration boundaries. Baseline: `nexus-v2-g2-development`, `5723e5fa6ce2824d94685ff2cd6e0742cd5715a7`, clean; source MANIFEST 173/173 matched.

**Proposed conclusion A: CURRENT G2 RESPONSIBILITY MODEL IS APPROPRIATELY SCOPED.** A1–A2 already delegate generic execution and allow application, qualified Host, or demonstrated combined enforcement. Scope & Exit §2 explicitly permits another Host to prove equivalent HQ1–HQ5 properties without reproducing LPAC APIs or the four Windows scenarios. Its §§4–9 already distinguish core threats, exact current local gates, replaceable observations, implementation details and future hardening. Route B §§3–5 distinguish documented candidacy from measured qualification and accept equivalent hard bounds, immutable/mutation-excluded policy evidence, and fail-closed unknown effects. No concrete accepted cross-route obligation presently requires reproducing PSS, Windows CI, mapped system DLL bytes, Job Objects or the local verifier on a remote Host.

This is an interpretation of unchanged accepted text, **not** permission to bypass an exact current protocol gate. If a future provider can preserve a required property only by changing a currently accepted evidence promise, that change needs a separately reviewed and owner-accepted versioned successor. Present provider documentation and current deployment evidence do not justify that successor or an experiment.

## 2. Classification method and responsibility layers

The requested one-of-five label is a **primary responsibility classification for each compound HQ/P row**, not a claim that every clause in that row has the same owner. **A** is Nexus-owned product property; **B** is delegable Host/platform property that Nexus must bind and verify; **C** is replaceable evidence mechanism; **D** is Host-specific legacy mechanism; **E** is future hardening. Individual C/D/E mechanisms inside A/B rows are listed in §5. No mandatory HQ/P security outcome is discarded by classifying a mechanism as C or D.

| Layer | Responsibility boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Model/intelligence | Propose reasoning or work; no authoritative grants, hashes, cost settlement, policy state or Host qualification. Model choice remains independent. |
| Agent/orchestration platform | May supply a mature loop, native sessions, tool calls, context management and continuation. Its state does not restore Nexus authority, settle exposure or certify effects. |
| Execution Host/provider | May enforce isolation, input and resource limits, native lifecycle and effect observations for an exact qualified mode. A provider contract/telemetry is evidence within its stated trust model, not self-certification of every applied restriction. |
| Nexus | Determines eligibility, validates independently issued and then-valid task authorization, current policy and accepted capability, governs sequential dispatch and budgets, preserves ordered domain facts, acceptance, uncertain effects and audit, binds and invalidates Host qualifications. Nexus need not rebuild a generic agent runtime, sandbox, scheduler, provider billing system or operating-system inspector. |

Delegation never turns a Host claim, plan limit, model output or Nexus record into an independently issued grant. The accepted threat model does not require proof against a malicious provider/kernel/fully trusted owner, hardware attestation, universal exactly-once effects or complete transcripts (Route B §5).

## 3. HQ1–HQ6 responsibility map

“Stronger?” flags a possible *misreading or local-only mechanism*, not a finding that the accepted abstract property is excessive. “Replace” always means an independently reviewed equivalent under the applicable versioned change gate; until then the exact local protocol still STOPs.

| Gate; primary class | Threat/property and current owner | Delegation; minimum sufficient evidence | Stronger mechanism / consequence of replacement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **HQ1 — B** execution provenance | Code, package or controller substitution. Host/OS enforces execution identity; Nexus binds the admitted operation and reviewed Nexus-controlled bytes/configuration to that identity. | An exact supported mode, stable unit and admitted workload/dependency identity, protected custody and subject-external observation sufficient to rule out substitution across the relevant check/use window. Provider-managed internals are a qualified TCB, not an obligation to hash every provider DLL. | Same-process Windows process handles, PDB pins and mapped-byte comparison are D/C outside their exact local protocol. A bare image tag, prelaunch hash or guest path cannot replace the binding; removing provenance would permit substituted execution. |
| **HQ2 — B** effective isolation | Untrusted work reaches owner secrets, unauthorized files/processes/network or policy controls. Host enforces; Nexus specifies and checks the boundary. | Exact effective restrictions, control-actor exclusion or safe drift rule, source/scope/freshness, and relevant negative observations for the admitted graph. A supported provider contract may anchor the platform TCB but stored desired settings alone do not prove applied state. | LPAC SID/integrity, empty capabilities and status-only CredReadW are D for another Host. Removing equivalent isolation allows secret/control escape. |
| **HQ3 — B** controlled inputs | Environment, file, mount, IPC, handle or side channel smuggles owner authority into execution. Host enforces; Nexus closes admitted scope. | Effective task-relevant input graph, no reachable control/model secrets, protected setup and observations from outside the untrusted subject. Closed nonapplicable channels need explicit justification. | Exact six-name environment, TEMP/TMP paths and Windows handle inventory are D outside the local mode. Removing the closed graph permits indirect bypass. |
| **HQ4 — B** containment/cancel | Descendant, background, stalled or orphan effects survive a stop/deadline. Host supplies lifecycle and hard applicable lifetime; Nexus stops new dispatch and retains unresolved effects/exposure. | Bound original unit and relevant descendants/effects; distinguish request, acknowledgement, terminal state, zero-live where required, controller-loss and deadline outcome. Test realistic failures at the actual Host boundary. | No-outer-Job, CREATE_SUSPENDED, Windows watchdog/Job and Scenarios 01–04 are D/B for the exact local route, not a remote API recipe. Replacing without an equivalent bound allows orphan effects/cost. |
| **HQ5 — A** evidence integrity | Child-authored, stale, forged or mismatched observations create false PASS. Nexus owns the protected receipt and adjudication boundary; Host/OS may supply externally sourced facts. | Bind source, identity, exact unit/operation/configuration/time, coverage and gaps; protect records against the untrusted subject; contradictions and missing mandatory observations STOP. | Windows owner ACL/file ID and exact fixed receipt paths are D for other Hosts. Universal signed hardware attestations are E. Removing source integrity makes all other gate decisions unreliable. |
| **HQ6 — A** governed capability binding | A direct/deferred operation lacks authority, hard relevant exposure bounds, effect references or safe retry. Nexus owns admission, reservation and reconciliation decisions; a qualified Host supplies enforcing/observable primitives. | One accepted capability and exact configuration; closed reachable operation graph, pre-effect authority, applicable hard resource/money bounds, native operation/effect references, cancel/late-effect query and explicit UNKNOWN stop. Qualification proves these primitives, not the G2 ledger. | Local PID, specific provider API and exact proof fixtures are replaceable. Removing the capability-level gate would turn an isolated sandbox into ungoverned dispatch. |

The current HQ1–HQ5 local LPAC status remains UNKNOWN/STOP. Different Host equivalence is allowed by Scope & Exit §2, but no status or authority transfers from the local route.

## 4. P1–P12 responsibility map

Every P row remains a **required candidacy interface obligation**, with measured/account-specific Q proof at its applicable precreation or prerelease gate. “Minimum” below is the task-scoped supported path, not documentary PASS for E2B, Modal or Daytona.

| Contract; primary class | Property / owner | Delegation and minimum evidence | Stronger-than-property risk; consequence of removal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **P1 — B** identity | Prevent wrong deployment, image or operation attribution; Host identifies, Nexus correlates. | Supported exact mode/runtime/config and stable unit/operation/result references; later bind admitted Nexus-controlled code/dependencies and actual execution to trusted observations. | Provider-internal loaded-module byte proof is not universal. Removing identity prevents acceptance/reconciliation. |
| **P2 — B** control/data split | Prevent workload or another actor relaxing policy; platform enforces, Nexus owns authority allocation. | Enumerate creation/mutation/exec/file/terminate actors and credential custody; supported denial/attenuation or equivalent actor exclusion, then verify. | Separate API products are not mandatory. Removing split gives untrusted work control-plane authority. |
| **P3 — B** effective restrictions | Prevent requested policy masquerading as applied policy; Host enforces, Nexus adjudicates. | Subject-external evidence for each required applied restriction, bound to instance/configuration/freshness, with relevant negatives; immutable controlled configuration, verified mutation exclusion or drift-safe observation may establish the no-change interval. | A particular revision API or signed receipt is not mandatory. Config-only proof would permit silent drift. |
| **P4 — A** admitted input graph | Prevent indirect execution and secret reachability; Nexus defines closed scope, Host restricts actual channels. | Enumerate reachable command/filesystem/env/mount/IPC/process/network/service paths; measure only applicable channels and confirm control/model secrets are unreachable. | Exhaustive provider internals or an irrelevant network negative are not required. Dropping reachable paths permits bypass. |
| **P5 — B** resource bounds | Prevent whole-unit resource/lifetime/output overrun; Host/platform enforces, Nexus chooses task bounds. | Hard nonrelaxable limits for each reachable billable or safety-relevant dimension, possibly composed with qualified application controls; source/scope and controller-loss consequences. | A universal native knob for every listed dimension is unnecessary if no such path is reachable or an equivalent hard composition covers it. Client timeout alone does not. |
| **P6 — A** hard exposure | Prevent the authorized Nexus-managed dispatch/operation from exceeding its owner exposure ceiling; Nexus reserves/refuses/settles, provider supplies enforceable monetary or finite-resource bound. | Identify all applicable charges and a hard ceiling or verified conservative all-in maximum, current account/headroom, shared actors where relevant, and residual objects; late usage remains outstanding. | **P7 — B** cancellation | Prevent live work/effects beyond stop; Host supplies exact-unit stop/query/failure bound, Nexus separates stop states. | Original unit and applicable descendants/background effects, independently enforceable non-workload-relaxable lifetime/failure bound and queryable request/ack/terminal facts, with lost-ack reconciliation. | Whole-tree tests are task-scoped: prove absence of a channel or contain it. | **P8 — B** observation | Prevent status or effect misattribution; Host/external system supplies observations, Nexus validates acceptance. | Stable unit/operation references, result/failure/task-effect observations, known retention/expiry and background coverage where reachable. | Universal transcript is E. | **P9 — A** unknown effects | Prevent duplicate side effects after interruption; Nexus owns retry decision, Host supplies queryable retained facts. | Preserve original references; query outcome/effects or establish positive no-effect evidence; otherwise retain UNKNOWN and stop. | Native idempotency key or snapshot is optional. Without invalidation stale evidence could falsely preserve binding eligibility; it never becomes authority. |

## 5. Mechanisms, finite minimum binding, and architecture impact

**Replaceable evidence mechanisms (C):** an exact zero-entry PSS table, a particular Windows debugger/PSS handle walk, raw mapped-byte equality for transformed Windows system modules, a signed policy receipt as the only proof, a provider policy-revision API as the only freshness mechanism, native idempotency as the only safe retry mechanism, or a monetary-cap API as the only hard exposure mechanism. Each replacement must preserve its threat coverage and pass the applicable accepted change gate; the current exact local STOPs remain operative.

**Host-specific legacy mechanisms (D):** LPAC SID/integrity/capability details, NexusTest history, Windows CredReadW, Job Object/no-outer-Job, Windows CI/PDB/module pins, exact local six-name environment and TEMP/TMP paths, sealed V4 and Scenario 01–04 implementation. The sealed package remains historical evidence; it is not a remote Host qualification packet.

**Future hardening (E):** provider-malicious/kernel proofs, hardware attestation as a universal condition, full transcripts, universal snapshots/exactly-once effects, all-provider coverage, recursive verifier chains, and exhaustive irrelevant dependency inventory. Promote none without a concrete uncovered G2 threat, affected product property, failure of existing controls and the minimum added control.

**Nexus-owned minimum:** independently issued authority validation before each dispatch/resume; capability/policy eligibility; sequential admission; reservation and uncertain-exposure accounting; protected domain evidence; artifact acceptance; UNKNOWN-effect reconciliation; revocation/drift invalidation. **Delegable platform minimum:** the exact execution boundary, nonrelaxable applicable isolation/resource/lifetime controls, native cancellation, and result/effect/usage observations. Nexus must verify and bind those delegated properties, without reproducing the platform engine.

**Proposed minimum Positive Host Binding (interpretation of existing accepted criteria):** one exact supported Host mode and already accepted capability; protected identity of the admitted controller/workload/configuration and original operation; HQ6 demonstrates complete pre-effect admission under a separately authorized test grant for reachable direct/deferred paths, but qualification itself confers no continuing task authority; effective isolation and secret/control-actor exclusion; hard applicable resource and total authorized operation-exposure bounds; bounded whole-unit cancellation or equivalent lifetime containment; trustworthy, sufficiently fresh source-bound result/effect/usage observations; durable references for checkpoint/recovery and ambiguous-effect/cost reconciliation; relevant negative and interruption tests; explicit STOP on a mandatory contradiction, unobserved channel or unresolved exposure. Each later dispatch/resume must revalidate a then-valid independently issued grant. HQ1–HQ5-equivalent boundary evidence and HQ6 capability primitives must both be positive. The first useful sequential G2 workflow and its ledger/fault-injection acceptance remain separate.

**Architecture impact:** No A0–A6, Roadmap, G1, R2/R4/R5, Scope & Exit, local protocol, next-route decision or Route B contract change is justified by this review. Wording ambiguities in P1/P5/P7/P10 should be resolved as task-scoped equivalence under the existing text, not by silently lowering a gate. If a future exact candidate cannot meet a currently literal accepted gate with an equivalent, propose a named versioned successor for that gate and prove unchanged safety before any experiment. No current provider becomes eligible from this interpretation; E2B/Modal/Daytona remain UNKNOWN. No Host is selected.
