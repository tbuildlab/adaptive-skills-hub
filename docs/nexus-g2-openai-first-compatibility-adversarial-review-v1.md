## 3. Safety-core re-test

The review preserves each required invariant:

- authority and reservation precede effects;
- workload cannot expand authority or receive controller credentials;
- reachable exposure remains finitely and enforceably bounded;
- stop/cancel/terminal/effect/cost states remain distinct;
- UNKNOWN is durable and blocks unsafe retry;
- evidence is not guest-only;
- qualification binds exact public configuration/operation/evidence scope and expires;
- material visible drift or missing evidence invalidates;
- default deny and qualification-before-execution remain.

No replacement invariant is weaker. The main availability trade-off is explicit: where provider idempotency/retention is absent, Nexus stops rather than retries. The main trust residual is also explicit: managed-service internal implementation changes may be invisible, so Nexus relies on the provider’s public contract for that layer and bounds qualification reuse; it does not claim internal attestation.

## 4. P5/P6 blocker challenge

The proposed clarification cannot justify an experiment while remote active work or charges can grow without a provider-enforced finite bound. Disabling multi-agent/tools/network narrows the graph but does not by itself bound root-agent model/tool-loop steps, retries, continuous sandbox activity or asynchronous cleanup charges. Published rates and best-effort usage remain insufficient.

Therefore the minimized six-question pack correctly keeps provider clarification for configuration mutation/network scope and, critically, whole-unit hard resource/lifetime bounds, managed loop bounds, complete charges and settlement tail. If OpenAI cannot provide a finite hard composition, the candidate remains blocked regardless of other simplifications.

## 5. Decision and scope verdict

Wholesale re-baselining would overstate the defect because accepted A0–A6 and the Responsibility Re-baseline already support composite guarantees. Leaving the workflow entirely unchanged would preserve an unnecessarily provider-only reading of internal identity, monolithic receipts, native idempotency and complete pre-experiment documentation.
