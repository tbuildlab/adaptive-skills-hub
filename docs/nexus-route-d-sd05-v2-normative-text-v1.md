# SD05 v2 proposed normative replacement

Document ID: nexus.route-d.sd05-v2-normative-proposal.v1
Status: PROPOSED ONLY. REQUIRES EXACT OWNER GOVERNANCE ACCEPTANCE.
This is not implementation permission or an issued grant.

## Applicability

N01. This contract MAY apply only to one separately admitted, Owner-attended,
secret-free VM-FREE LAB_ONLY synthetic marker with an exact fixed effect graph,
sole protected consumer and finite invocation/object/byte reservations. It MUST
NOT supply real attendance, task authority, VM control, provider/network access,
production evidence, HQ PASS, Host qualification or G2 acceptance. A separate
applicability and residual-risk decision MUST identify this exact version/hash.

N02. An independently issued grant for the exact future fixture MUST explicitly
permit v2 domain-ordered expiry, the one-shot logical commitment and residual
invocation/completion exposure. Its external thresholds remain original observed
limits; they are not claimed as atomic authority cutoff timestamps. An
expired grant MUST NOT be refreshed, replayed, converted into a new grant or
extended by C. No existing expired grant is grandfathered. If any applicable
law/platform/governance/issuer obligation requires real-time effect/commit/cessation
cutoff or finite all-fault exposure, this contract is inapplicable and MUST refuse.

## Identity, custody and original

N03. Before eligibility, independently verify every admitted actor/backend path,
executing code/dependencies, public/authority/clock reference, process/token/handle,
endpoint, ledger/checkpoint, eligible namespace and observer custody. Only C may
invoke/accept the eligible marker. U cannot replace, replay, write or bypass any
of those planes. SD01 UNKNOWN and any mandatory custody UNKNOWN MUST block all
implementation/setup/release progression. A different SID or service is not proof.

N04. Exactly one independently issued original/descriptor is allowed. Preparatory
ADMIT is not a dispatch permit. The exact immutable intent and the irreversible
slot/reservation consumption MUST have accepted persistence evidence before any
external marker invocation. Once intent is attempted, uncertain persistence MUST
make the slot unavailable; no reset, refund, automatic resume, retry or replacement.
Storage writes required for intent/commit/evidence are separately enumerated,
authorized and reserved; they are not hidden zero-cost actions.

## Logical order and expiry

N05. One exclusively protected state orders all authenticated requests and
terminal decisions for that original. COMMIT_L means the terminal, irrevocable
logical decision to attempt only this exact marker once. CLOSE_L means a terminal
revocation, expiry or mandatory-stop decision. Only one can win from the pending
state. After COMMIT_L, a later close event MUST still be recorded and close all
other admission; it does not erase or replace the original winning decision.
A single fixed consumer can serialize these transitions with a documented
memory synchronization primitive; that primitive is not storage/clock atomicity
or proof of SD01. No FIFO, fairness or bounded response-time claim follows.

N06. Owner request, authenticated domain receipt, logical transition, persistence,
physical submission/mutation/completion and cessation MUST be recorded separately.
A receipt/queued timer MUST NOT be acknowledged as effective closure. A close
acknowledgement requires its exact protected sequence and required successful
persistence; unknown acknowledgement delivery remains UNKNOWN. A COMMIT_L acknowledgement
requires successful commit-record persistence, but still proves neither invocation
nor completion. No recovered commit record ever authorizes dispatch after restart.

N07. Before a candidate COMMIT_L, C MUST evaluate authentic fresh UTC and original
monotonic/phase samples, the earliest original deadline, accepted attendance
predicate and all authority/custody/reservation/observation checks. A due deadline,
known revocation/attendance loss, contradiction, drift or decisive UNKNOWN observed
in that protected decision MUST close admission and prevent COMMIT_L. A timer is
only a prompt to perform this decision, not the enforcing point. No automatic
grant extension is allowed. Record sample sources, epochs, observation intervals,
freshness basis, policy/deadlines and actual sequence.

N08. External expiry is the original numeric UTC/monotonic boundary.
EXPIRE_L is the protected terminal transition ordering expiry. They MUST NOT be
equated. A valid sample followed by delay can precede a later COMMIT_L after
external expiry but before EXPIRE_L; this is an explicitly accepted v2 residual
risk, not a timely commitment. No finite sample-to-transition or transition-to-I/O
bound is claimed. The original grant's external deadlines remain visible and
MUST NOT be changed. Observed lateness/plan breaches are retained, not relabelled.

N09. If CLOSE_L wins before COMMIT_L, no commitment or eligible marker invocation
may follow. If COMMIT_L wins, no later event may create a different commitment or
reset the original. Revocation closes every other admission and is recorded as
affecting a previously committed original, with residual outcome separate. The
exact original may be abandoned; commit does not compel an invocation.

## One eligible invocation and residual completion

N10. Successful persistence of the exact COMMIT_L record, after required intent
persistence, is mandatory before eligible marker invocation. Failure/unknown
persistence forbids that invocation and leaves the slot consumed/unavailable.
This is persist-before-effect, not an atomic file/ledger transaction. Physical
intent/effect/result partial writes remain explicit. No all-fault durability is
inferred from API return, flush or hashes.

N11. Only the same live C processing the original may make at most one application
invocation on the same held fixed marker capability. No caller receives a permit,
handle or deferred callback; no queue of future effects, child, batch, renewed
lease, reopened consumer or recovery process may use it. A predeclared bounded
native operation and effect graph must cover all consequential metadata/storage
work; opaque indirect effects make the candidate ineligible. A new call after
an uncertain first call is a retry and is forbidden.

N12. If COMMIT_L already won, the original one invocation may be physically
submitted, mutate its marker or complete after external expiry or ordered close,
ONLY under separately accepted original residual-completion scope. This includes
delay before native call entry, not merely pending kernel I/O. It MUST NOT be
described as 'no new physical effect after expiry'. Any newly known custody,
descriptor, capability, attendance/observer failure or contradiction before
invocation MUST instead abandon it and retain consumed state. If loss occurs
after I/O issue, attempt only separately authorized original cancellation/stop;
do not infer that those requests undo or prevent completion. No pause/resume or
restart is authorized to preserve this allowance.

N13. Completion allowance is not a numeric time bound. Planned deadline/margin
misses close further activity and remain violations or UNKNOWN as applicable.
Controller/OS/kernel/storage failure tails, ongoing resources and cessation may
remain UNKNOWN under a separately accepted engineering fault scope only. No
positive qualification or finite tail claim is allowed. Where an absolute cap
includes those tails, admission MUST be refused rather than exceeded or waived.

## Evidence, failure and acceptance

N14. Evidence MUST bind source/scope/omissions/time/original/sequence, exact
consumer/image/object/descriptor, consumed intent, logical commit/close order,
native submission and observed outcome. Bounds/UNKNOWN MUST replace unobservable
physical mutation instants. File last-write time, child output, hash, prior sample
or timer signal cannot certify effect timeliness. Authentic independent observers
must be outside all U write/process/control/substitution authority.

N15. Crash, unknown delivery/persistence/effect, clock epoch change, observer loss,
contradiction or drift stops admission and preserves original exposure. Reconcile
only under its own valid authority. No inference of no effect, no automatic
cleanup, effect replay or reservation reuse. Cleanup needs separate authority,
positive original cessation and verified inverses; evidence is never rolled back.

N16. Acceptance of these words does not accept SD01, domain setup, credentials,
attendance source, numeric U03/U04/budget, code, an experiment, a Host or G2. Exact
successor architecture/setup/applicability acceptance, supported concrete custody,
adversarial/static review and separately issued implementation/setup/test/release
decisions remain mandatory. LAB_ONLY lineage and derivatives are permanently
inadmissible for positive HQ/Host/production evidence. A0-A6, P1-P12, HQ1-HQ6,
production Section 7, E01-E12 and EO-AUTONOMY-01 accepted bytes remain unchanged;
any local E10 exception must be separately explicit and must not alter production.
